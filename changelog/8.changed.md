`qdac.play`'s `dwell` is now an integer number of microseconds, default `2`, and `qdac.play`'s `delay` and `qdac.set_trigger`'s `duration` are now seconds, as a `float`: `play(bus, wf, dwell=2, delay=1e-3)` and `set_trigger(bus, 10e-6, outputs={1})`. The `qdac-default-v1` profile, now at version 0.2.0, rejects a `dwell` under the QDAC-II's floor of 2 microseconds with a `qdac.dwell-below-minimum` error, and its `min_dwell_ns: 100` limit becomes `min_dwell_us: 2`. No migration is registered, so a `.qp` file written with 0.2 still loads and its numbers are read in the new units; rebuild it from its program instead.
