`qdac.play`'s `repetitions` now defaults to `None`, meaning not given: the platform that runs the program chooses the count, where it used to default to `1`. A `.qp` file that omits `repetitions` loads with `None`, and a play with `repetitions=1` now writes it.

`qdac.set_trigger` and `SetTrigger` take `outputs` before `position`, `(bus, duration, outputs, position)`, and `outputs` accepts one int as well as an iterable of them. A call that passes `position` by position, and not by name, needs its two last arguments swapped.
